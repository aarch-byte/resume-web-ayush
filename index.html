Top 10 Finalist • National Round IIM Bangalore Offline Finals 14 November 2026
Role: Marketing & Financial Modelling (Team of 5)

MITTI Compass: Sustainable Social Enterprise Expansion

National finalist strategic blueprint for MITTI Café — a social enterprise employing persons with disabilities. The objective was to design a scalable commercial expansion model across corporate and campus locations without exposing the enterprise to punitive commercial rental debt or mission drift.

01. Strategic Problem Context

How to scale café presence across metro corporate hubs while avoiding heavy commercial rent liabilities, mitigating high upfront CapEx, and preserving the brand's core mission of dignified employment for persons with disabilities.

Core Challenge Commercial Rents & Fixed Debt Risk

02. Financial & Strategic Architecture

  • Mobile Cart CapEx: Modeled ₹2,50,000 unit costs for rapid deployment in corporate tech complexes and academic institutions.
  • Automated POS Debt Deduction: Modeled ₹2,500 monthly recovery tied to daily Point-of-Sale receipts, achieving 98.4% modeled debt recovery with zero fixed-default risk.
  • Institutional Hosted Model: 60–70% corporate-hosted café ratio to eliminate expensive commercial real estate rentals.
  • Prudent Depreciation: 20% Written Down Value (WDV) method applied with ring-fenced operational risk governance.
  • Inclusive Brand Narrative: Inclusive superhero comic mascot concepts and mobile cart pop-ups.
